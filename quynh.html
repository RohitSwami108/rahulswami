Portfolio of Quynh Cao (Zizi), a designer in Vietnam with 7 years across product, web, motion and design systems.

Selected work

Chat Artify

Mobile Application · 2025

Project for an AI-powered chatbot platform that seamlessly integrates intelligent content creation using Midjourney and ChatGPT.

RunX Ring

Mobile Application · 2025

RunX Ring is a wearable fitness tracker worn on your finger. It pairs with your smartphone or tablet via the RunX App, automatically tracking your daily activity and syncing the data in real time.

Monster Chat App

Mobile Application · 2021

Monster Messenger is an iOS UI kit that brings messaging, social, and mobile payments together in one app. A major update to the original Chat App UI Kit, it has over 100 screens in light and dark mode and covers nearly every kind of message, from text, photos, and video to files, audio, and polls, ready to start a new app or drop into an existing one.

Travala Travel

Web Application · 2025

A travel booking platform for hotels and flights, designed to make finding and booking a trip feel clear and effortless. Travala works alongside RunX Ring: the tokens users earn by staying active become a way to pay for where they go next.

Hyundai TC Motor

Web Design · 2023

A complete website redesign for Hyundai TC Motor in Vietnam, covering vehicle browsing, model comparisons, test-drive bookings, dealer search, and after-sales services.

Yamaha Revzone

Web Design · 2022

Revzone is Yamaha's official home for big bikes in Vietnam, but its website no longer matched the energy of the machines on its floor. This redesign rebuilds the experience around the ride: dramatic model pages, clear pricing across six lines, riding gear and apparel, and a simple path to service at Revzone.

Trip.Social

Web Design · 2023

Trip.social is an experience booking platform for tours, day trips and travel services across Vietnam, Australia, New Zealand and Singapore. I redesigned the whole website, from the home page and destination pages to tour listings and booking. The goal was a cleaner, more inspiring way to discover local experiences and a smoother path from browsing to checkout, across three languages and on any screen.
